## Final result

- **Live app:** https://iafl-crewpulse.onrender.com
- **Source code:** https://github.com/yusufsahin-student/IAFL_crewPulse
- **Outputs:** Project Documentation (TR and EN), project presentation and demo video in the team's Google Drive folder.

CrewPulse helps an astronaut far from Earth notice health changes early and know what to do. Its advice changes with the distance from Earth: on the ISS the ground team decides, during Mars transit the crew acts first.

## Next steps after the hackathon

- Add the Mars surface as a mission setting.
- Test the personal normal model with real or published health data.
- Review the health limits and advice with flight surgeons and physicians.
- Add camera-based measurements and connections to medical devices.
- Adapt the same idea for places on Earth that are far from doctors.
